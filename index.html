01. Intact Bladder Diffusion Nominal Regime

Solution-Diffusion in Polyurethane Elastomers

In intact multi-layer pressure bladders, pure oxygen permeation proceeds through microscopic polymer free volume via steady-state Henry-Fick transport. The mass flow rate $\dot{m}_{\text{perm}}$ scales strictly linearly with differential pressure $\Delta P$:

$$\dot{m}_{\text{perm}} = \frac{\mathcal{P}_{\text{eff}} \cdot A}{L} \cdot \Delta P = \frac{S \cdot D \cdot A}{L} (P_{\text{suit}} - P_{\text{lunar}})$$

Where $\mathcal{P}_{\text{eff}}$ is the effective permeability coefficient ($S \cdot D$, solubility times diffusivity), $A$ is active bladder surface area, and $L$ is bladder wall thickness ($\sim 280\ \mu\text{m}$). The gradient remains constant: $\frac{\partial \dot{m}}{\partial P} = \frac{S \cdot D \cdot A}{L} = \text{const}$.

02. Razor Puncture Hydrodynamics Hazard Anomaly

Choked Orifice Flow & Quadratic Escape Dynamics

When sub-millimeter jagged lunar shards puncture the outer polyurethane matrix, bulk hydrodynamic escape dominates over molecular diffusion. For sonic or choked gas outflow across pressure ratio $P_0/P > 1.89$ into lunar vacuum:

$$\dot{m}_{\text{leak}} = C_d \cdot A_{\text{hole}} \cdot P_0 \sqrt{\frac{\gamma M}{R T_0}} \left(\frac{2}{\gamma + 1}\right)^{\frac{\gamma+1}{2(\gamma-1)}} \propto C_d A_{\text{hole}} P^{\alpha}$$

In unchoked and transitional regimes, hydrodynamic flow scales nonlinearly as $\dot{m} \propto \sqrt{2\rho \Delta P}$ or produces distinct exponential step jumps with high instantaneous slopes ($\alpha > 1.8$), allowing instantaneous demarcation from natural thermal/pressure drifts.
